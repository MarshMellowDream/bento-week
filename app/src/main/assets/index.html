Bento Week.

Your whole day on one page: shower, chores, work, games, meals and bed. Bedtime steps earlier every 3 days until it reaches 10 PM. A machine runs every other day, laundry one time and the dishwasher the next, and never during the 4–9 PM peak power hours.

The week

Cooking starts Monday, Oct 19 with Week A. Four weeks rotate (A, B, C, D), each with different proteins, sides, onigiri and weekend meals, then it starts over. The dot marks this week; tap a letter to see what's coming. Meal times follow your wake-up time in the day plan above.

Monday prep

About 75 minutes. No rice to cook: your microwave rice bowls are shelf-stable, so you only prep the protein, sides and onigiri. It starts half an hour after lunch and finishes before peak power hours begin at 4. Sunday works too if Monday gets busy.

    Food safety: cooked meat and salmon keep 3–4 days in the fridge, so Monday's cooking is good through Thursday. Onigiri firm up when cold; 20–30 seconds in the microwave fixes it. Sealed rice bowls stay in the pantry until you open them.
